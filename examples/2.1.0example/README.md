# Effect Collection for XP Orb Trails 2.1.0

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Download the ZIP](https://github.com/GoldenEggOVO/xp_orb_trails/raw/refs/heads/26.3/examples/2.1.0example/2.1.0example.zip)

Put `2.1.0example.zip` in `config/xp_orb_trails/packs/`. In **Profiles → File Profiles**, select **Reload Files**, then click one of the 24 profiles labeled with the ZIP filename. Try the following in your world.

## Orb bodies and pixel art

| Profile | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Butterfly Homecoming | Follow a moth as it sheds pixels, then collect it to see the pixels gather into wings. |
| River Procession | Lead orbs along a shallow channel with flowing water and obstacles. Watch the school steer around them and the motes follow the current. |
| Pixel Relief | View the slowly turning brass gear from the side to see the depth of its teeth. |
| Painted Fracture | Pick up the lantern. Its painted surface breaks into pieces that turn as they fly apart. |

## Trails and motion

| Profile | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Ink Brush | Move in a straight line, then turn sharply. Compare the fine stroke with the wider bend. |
| Shifting Cross Section | View the trail from the side to see its cross-section change from a circle to a star and a flat ribbon. |
| Returning Rings | Let an orb travel before collecting it. Its rings light up and expand in sequence. |
| Eight-view Skiff | Walk around the spacecraft to see its directional sprite change with your viewing angle. |
| Articulated Wings | Watch the wingtips lag behind the wing roots as the tail swings. |
| Light to Hand | Pick up an orb and follow the golden spark as it arcs into your hand. |

## Collisions and nearby blocks

| Profile | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Crossing Sparks | Make the paths of two orbs cross in quick succession to leave a spark at the intersection. |
| Merging Lights | Bring two orbs close together to see an amber bridge form between them. |
| Redstone Pulse | Lead an orb past powered redstone, then cut the power to change its surrounding glow. |
| Second Bounce Snow | Collect an orb over a solid floor. Its ice particles release snow dust on the second bounce. |
| Surface Prints | Compare a pickup over solid ground with one near water. Look for ink spots on the ground and rings on the water. |

## Scenes

| Profile | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Orbital Observatory | Look across the tilted rings of the little planet, then collect it for a burst of stars. |
| Lantern Swarm | Follow the fireflies at night and watch their lights gather at pickup. |
| Magnetic Storm | Make a sharp turn and look for scattered fragments and branching arcs. |
| Tideglass | Collect an orb near water to see droplets stretch and ripples spread at different times. |
| Frostwake | Let an ice crystal leave a trail, then collect it over a hard floor to see the fragments bounce. |
| Spring Flight | Alternate standing still and running to see the bird switch between hovering and gliding. Collect it for falling petals. |
| Dusk Moth | Try the moth outdoors at dusk or in rain to see the wings, ribbon and weather interact. |
| Ember Mirage | View the orb against a textured wall to pick out the heat distortion around the embers. |
| Glowstone Beacon | Lead the crystal toward a dark wall or floor to see its warm halo. |

To edit an effect, change its JSON in the ZIP’s `profiles/` folder. Keep the same folder layout and ZIP filename, reload files and select the profile again.
